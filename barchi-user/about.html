About Barchi Furniture

Crafting Timeless Elegance Since 1998

Welcome to Barchi Furniture — India’s premier destination for handcrafted plantation solid teak wood furniture, bespoke living room suites, and luxury custom interior solutions.

Our Heritage & Craftsmanship

Founded on the principles of uncompromising quality and timeless woodworking artistry, Barchi Furniture brings together generations of master woodcarvers and modern architectural designers. Every piece of furniture in our collection is forged from 100% kiln-dried solid teak and sheesham wood, treated against moisture and termite degradation.

Why Barchi Stands Apart

Unlike mass-produced particle board alternatives, Barchi creations are heirloom-grade pieces built to last lifetimes. From high-resilience memory foam cushioning wrapped in Belgian velvet to precision Italian marble dining surfaces, we curate only finest raw materials worldwide.
