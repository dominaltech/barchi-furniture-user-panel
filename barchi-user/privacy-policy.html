Privacy Policy

How we collect, protect, and use your personal information

1. Information We Collect

When you make a purchase or attempt to make a purchase through Barchi Furniture, we collect certain information from you, including:

  • Order Information: Name, billing address, shipping address, and payment information.
  • Contact Information: Email address and phone number.
  • Device Information: We automatically collect certain information about your device, including your web browser, IP address, and time zone.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the Order Information to fulfill any orders placed through the site, which includes:

  • Processing your payment information securely.
  • Arranging for shipping (sharing your address with our logistics partners).
  • Providing you with invoices and/or order confirmations.
  • Communicating with you about your order status.

3. Sharing Your Information

We share your Personal Information with third parties only to help us use your information as described above:

  • Logistics Partners: We share your name, address, and phone number with our delivery partners.
  • Payment Processors: Your payment details are processed through secure, encrypted gateways; we do not store your full credit card details on our servers.

4. Data Retention

When you place an order through Barchi Furniture, we will maintain your Order Information for our records unless and until you ask us to delete this information.

5. Security

We take reasonable precautions and follow industry best practices to make sure your personal information is not inappropriately lost, misused, accessed, disclosed, altered, or destroyed.

6. Your Rights

If you would like to access, correct, amend, or delete any personal information we have about you, please contact us at support@barchifurniture.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time in order to reflect changes to our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons.

Contact Us: For more information about our privacy practices, please contact us at support@barchifurniture.com.
