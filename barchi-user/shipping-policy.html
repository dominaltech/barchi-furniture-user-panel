Shipping Policy

Delivery timelines, rates, tracking, and protective packaging guidelines

1. Delivery Timeframe

Standard Shipping: All orders are processed and shipped via our courier partners. Please allow 7–10 business days for your product to be delivered to your doorstep.

Processing Time: Orders are typically prepared for dispatch within 24–48 hours of your purchase. You will receive an email confirmation once your order is on its way.

2. Shipping Rates

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on the weight of your order and your delivery location.

We occasionally offer promotional free shipping on orders above a certain value—keep an eye on our homepage for updates!

3. Order Tracking

Once your order has been shipped, we will provide you with a Tracking Number via email. You can use this to monitor your shipment's progress in real-time.

4. Packaging & Safety

Because our products are handcrafted solid wood furniture and luxury interior pieces, we use specialized, eco-friendly protective packaging to prevent breakage and maintain quality during the 7–10 day transit period.

5. Address Accuracy

Please ensure your shipping address is entered correctly. Barchi Furniture is not responsible for delayed or undelivered packages due to incorrect or incomplete address information provided at the time of checkout.

6. Damaged or Defective Goods

If your package arrives damaged during the 7–10 day journey, please take a photo immediately and contact us at support@barchifurniture.com within 24 hours of delivery. We will prioritize sending you a fresh replacement.
